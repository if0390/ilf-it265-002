\#### Learnings

\- Walking through actual turns in your head reveals problems that sound fine on paper — "What does Player 2 see when Player 1 does X?"

\- Card effects need to make sense for the game format — "reveal location" works in video games with hidden positions, but in a board game everyone already sees where tokens are



\#### Free Thinking

\- What if there was a "Purify" card that let the Queen reduce corruption? Would add more agency but might make corruption too easy to avoid

\- The Twins mechanic where they might be on opposite teams is really interesting — one could be secretly betraying the other the whole game

\- For future versions: could add more archetypes for 6-8 player games — maybe a Jester who wins by getting voted out, or a Knight who can protect others from elimination

\- The Blind Round Trickster card creates a mini-game within the game — everyone playing face-down adds a whole different kind of tension
