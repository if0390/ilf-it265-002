&nbsp; - Compared movement system to Clue instead of Monopoly — moving between rooms/locations, not counting spaces

&nbsp; - Broke down the turn structure into simple steps: Move → Play Card → Draw



\#### Learnings

\- Board games don't have to be like Monopoly — there are many different movement/board styles

\- Clue-style movement (room to room) works better for story-driven games than track-based movement

\- Video game mechanics CAN translate to board games — secret identities, status effects, dual-state systems

\- Getting feedback early reveals problems — all three testers had trouble understanding rules

\- Visual mockups help communicate ideas better than just describing them

\- Songs and other media can inspire game mechanics — "Alice of Human Sacrifice" shaped the entire theme

\- A game needs to be explainable in simple terms or players won't engage with it
